# Visual & gameplay reference notes

These references were reviewed for broad genre principles only. Doll Hunt uses its own room layout, procedural materials, colour palette, models and UI; it does not copy another game's maps, code or assets.

## References reviewed

1. **Propnight — Kitchen map preview**  
   https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1549180/view/3686799274263039898  
   Useful visual principle: familiar, detailed room props and practical warm lights inside an otherwise moody environment. Doll Hunt adaptation: warm lamp pools, darkened wood/brick/tile textures, and dense furniture silhouettes.

2. **Witch It — Official game page**  
   https://store.steampowered.com/app/559650/Witch_It/  
   Useful gameplay principle: hiding through environmental mimicry and giving hunters tools to detect, trap or slow hidden players. Doll Hunt adaptation: natural muted camouflage colours and a readable direction/signal clue rather than a precise location marker.

3. **Midnight Ghost Hunt — Beginner's Guide**  
   https://midnightghosthunt.com/beginners-guide  
   Useful gameplay principle: hiding inside ordinary furniture and giving hunters dedicated tracking devices/perks. Doll Hunt adaptation: furniture hiding for offline/practice modes, directional Doll Signal, dark atmospheric interiors and a high-contrast search tool flow.

## Design choices

- Dark blue-charcoal ambient base; warm amber practical lights and cool blue window/roof spill preserve colour readability.
- 9.4m wall/ceiling height opens vertical hiding space; ceiling attach uses a first-hit upward ray to respect occlusion.
- Doll surface colour is muted by default and has dark wood, moss, brick, concrete, steel and deep-teal presets.
- Eyes use layered warm sclera, brown iris, pupil and catchlights so they read as glass/porcelain eyes rather than two flat black dots.
- High graphics remains the default; MEDIUM/LOW remain available for thermal/FPS limits on mobile devices.

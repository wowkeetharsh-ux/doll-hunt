# Doll Hunt v23 — Colorful Home Edition

## User-requested visual direction
Replace the previous dark-stealth environment with the supplied colorful 3D home-room reference. The target look is cream floral wallpaper, cream lower wainscoting with raised decorative panels, turquoise/lime windows, warm wooden trim and rich wood flooring.

## Changes
- Rebuilt procedural wood-floor texture as warm honey oak with visible planks and grain.
- Rebuilt wallpaper texture as a repeating cream floral-vine design with sage leaves and muted rose/gold flowers.
- Rebuilt accent brick texture using light terracotta/rose bricks and pale mortar.
- Rebuilt kitchen tiles as pale aqua/ivory ceramic squares.
- Rebuilt ceiling panels in warm ivory.
- Recoloured room trim, wainscot panels, beams, pipes, plant pots, rugs, cabinetry, metal props and cushions to a lighter coordinated palette.
- Added matching lower-panel wainscoting to the four interior divider walls.
- Rebuilt windows with paired turquoise and lime panes, warm wooden frames and light gold mullions.
- Increased ambient, hemisphere and sun lighting; changed environment/fog to bright neutral tones.
- Increased exposure across LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/ULTRA presets without lowering render resolution.
- Added a Floral Wallpaper material/pattern to Paint Studio and updated surface sample metadata.
- Updated start-menu subtitle to COLORFUL 3D PROP HUNT ARENA.

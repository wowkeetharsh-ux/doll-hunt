# Seeker Hammer Test Checklist

1. Start Practice Room; confirm the action button says HAMMER and the hammer is visible in first-person.
2. Move near an almirah and search it; when a Doll is exposed, move within hammer reach and press HAMMER to tag it.
3. Try from beyond hammer reach; it must not tag.
4. Start Offline Doll Mode, hide in an almirah, and confirm a dwarf-knight Seeker walks to the cupboard, inspects it, swings its hammer and finds the Doll.
5. Online: confirm remote Seekers appear as dwarf knights, move with Walk/Idle animation and broadcast hammer swings.
6. Confirm the game remains usable if GLB loading fails (fallback hammer and existing AI visuals remain).

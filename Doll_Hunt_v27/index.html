HOW TO PLAY

  • ONLINE MATCH:
    Online matchmaking waits 15 seconds for real players, then fills up to 8 players with AI. Full match roles: 3 Dolls and 5 Seekers. New rooms are created automatically when a room fills.
  • OFFLINE DOLL MODE:
    You are the Doll for the whole round. Hide for 30 seconds; 4 AI Seekers search for you for 3 minutes. You never become a Seeker in this mode.
  • CAMOUFLAGE:
    Attach to a wall, high beam or ceiling, use the wall climb arrows to move up and down while attached, choose a colour and surface design pattern, and close or open your Doll's eyes. The arena has tall 9.4m walls and a high ceiling; after attaching, your Doll body stays fixed while the camera can freely move around the room.
  • FURNITURE HIDING:
    In Offline Doll Mode, hide inside the almirah in each room, or crouch under the table. AI Seekers must walk to the spot, open it and search for several seconds instead of catching you instantly.
  • CAMOUFLAGE SKILL:
    Match your Doll colour to the nearby wall/object, close your eyes and use half height. Matching colour, closed eyes and smaller height reduce the AI detection range.
  • PRACTICE MATCH:
    Choose 5–8 total players and Easy, Hard, Extreme or Impossible. The same team split as Online is used: 5=3 Hiders/2 Seekers, 6=4/2, 7=4/3, 8=5/3. You play one Seeker; the remaining slots are local AI bots.
  • PRACTICE SEARCH:
    Follow the directional Doll Signal, move close to one of the almirahs in each room and press E or tap SEARCH. Inspect the cabinet, then move close and hammer each exposed Hider. Extreme and Impossible Hiders can relocate and update their camouflage.
  • PAINT STUDIO:
    Choose a surface colour and design, then APPLY FINISH to cover the Doll or BRUSH to paint selected areas. MATCH SURFACE and SAMPLE copy a nearby surface's colour and material pattern.
  • HALF HEIGHT:
    Use the HALF HEIGHT button or press C to shrink the Doll's height to 50% without moving its position.
  • PRACTICE ROOM:
    Choose 5–8 total players and Easy, Hard, Extreme or Impossible AI. The same team split is used: 5 players = 3 Hiders / 2 Seekers; 8 players = 5 Hiders / 3 Seekers. You play as one Seeker, the other slots are AI teammates and several AI Hiders hide in different cabinets. Inspect each spot, expose a Hider, then get close and swing the hammer.
